Spectra Render

Valor Cat Studios presents realtime wave optics for Blender. Spectral, polarized and unbiased by construction, live in Blender's viewport on any ray-tracing GPU.

Visit spectra-render.com Join the beta

Diamond Body. Dispersion fire through cut crystal and thin-film colour on a soap bubble, traced wavelength by wavelength.

Light, solved as a wave.

Maxwell is the substrate, not a post-process. Interference, diffraction and polarization are computed per beam rather than painted on.

Spectral, always
Every path carries four wavelengths across the visible band, along with its full polarization state. There are no RGB approximations in the transport.
Fire in every facet
Wavelength-dependent index of refraction splits white light into rainbows. Diamonds sparkle because the physics says so.
An addon, not a fork
Install a zip into stock Blender. Spectra reads Principled-based materials, lights, cameras and colour management, with nothing to convert.
